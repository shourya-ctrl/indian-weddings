# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Couples planning their own Indian wedding, at home or as a destination wedding. They are the primary users: they find vendors and venues, shape the budget and guest list, build the wedding website and run the day, with Noor alongside them. Families and professional planners are not a confirmed audience.

## Product Purpose

IndianWeddings helps a couple plan a wedding that feels like theirs without the plan becoming a second job. It brings finding vendors and venues, the budget, guests, the wedding website and the run sheet into one plan, with an AI advisor who knows what the couple doesn't yet. Success is more clarity for the couple, and more room to enjoy the celebration.

## Positioning

- **Noor inside the plan.** Noor is an AI advisor that works across the couple's whole plan (budget, guests, vendors, the day itself) and answers from it, not a chat window off to the side.
- **Built for Indian weddings.** A wedding here is several celebrations (welcome party, haldi, mehendi, sangeet, baraat, pheras, reception), each with its own place, time, look and guests, planned as one connected whole.
- **Everything in one place.** Vendors, budget, guests, website and run sheet are connected rather than scattered across tools.

## Operating Context

- The couple discovers IndianWeddings on this landing page; "Start planning" and "Sign in" lead to the couples web app (`IndianWeddings Couples Web App V2`, not part of this repository).
- Planning runs from first vendor search through the save-the-date to the day itself, including day-of updates to guests.
- The advisor's display name is configurable (Noor, Advisor, Best friend, Co-planner); Noor is the default.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Shown on the landing page as the product's capabilities (pre-launch; not yet confirmed as built):

- Noor, the AI advisor, answering from the couple's own plan.
- Open dates: venues offering dates they would rather fill, at a better price. Shown on the page; not confirmed as a positioning claim.
- Vendor and venue discovery (venues, photography, décor and florals, food and drink).
- Budget, run sheet and guest planning; a wedding website with a page and look per event; posters made from the couple's photographs; day-of alerts to guests.

Constraints:

- This repository is the marketing landing page only (Vite + React).
- The couples app the page links to lives elsewhere.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: IndianWeddings.
- Advisor name: Noor ("Noor means light").
- Voice on the current page: warm, plain and confident, speaking to the couple as "you" (e.g. "A wedding that feels like you. A plan that holds it all.").

## Evidence on Hand

The product is pre-launch. There is no live product, no real vendors, no real couples and no testimonials yet.

- Vendor listings are sample listings.
- Noor's advice is sample advice, written to show how Noor works.
- "Real weddings" stories are illustrative.
- Photography is by PTaufiq Photography, used for design review only. Those are not IndianWeddings couples, and the photos are not licensed for launch.
- Future work must not present any of these as real customers, results, partners or reviews, and must not invent pricing, metrics or press.

## Product Principles

1. The couple's own plan is the source of truth; Noor and every feature work from it.
2. Treat an Indian wedding as many celebrations that belong to one plan.
3. Fewer tools, not more: connect what couples would otherwise juggle.
4. Clarity over pressure: show what matters next, and leave room to enjoy it.
5. Never fabricate proof while the product is pre-launch.
