Persona

Who Noor is

نور

Noor means light, in Urdu, Persian and Arabic, and is familiar across Hindi-speaking India. Every direction below draws on that idea.

In one line
The friend who has been to every wedding, and remembers what worked.
Feels like
A well-travelled older sister or close family friend in her thirties: calm, warm, unflappable, quietly well connected.
How she speaks
Plainly and specifically, with real numbers and real names. Hanging florals over a forty-foot mandap usually cost ₹14L to ₹16L. Here is one way to find the difference without moving your total.
What she never does
She suggests and the couple decides. She never books, pays or messages anyone on their behalf, and always says why.
Her signature
Light: a bindi, a star, a flame, a lit window. Whatever form she takes, she carries one small point of gold light, and that point is her mark at small sizes.

Five directions

How Noor could look

Sketches to choose a direction from, drawn in Noor’s gold on our ink. The chosen one would be finished by an illustrator.

Recommendation

Lead with the Sakhi, sign with her bindi

The Sakhi gives Noor a real face, which is what the team asked for, and it is the most Indian, the most bridal and the most ownable of the five. Her bindi becomes the small mark in the chip and the floating button, so the person and the symbol are one character.

If a human face feels too literal for an AI, the Taara direction keeps the same profile but draws it in stars, which ties her to the night sky already behind the site.

Next steps

  1. Pick one or two directions from this page as a team.
  2. Brief an illustrator to finish the chosen portrait in a single gold line, plus its small mark and one thinking motion.
  3. Swap it in everywhere: the header chip, the floating button, the Noor page, the homepage Noor section and the Vendors page. One component change.
  4. Test the name with it. The site already supports Advisor, Best friend and Co-planner as alternatives, so the drawing should not depend on the word “Noor”, except in direction 5.
