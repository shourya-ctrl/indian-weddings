Recommendation

Keep the Sakhi, or move to the Sutradhar

The Sakhi is still the best match for “the friend who has been to every wedding”: warm, peer-to-peer and the most aspirational for the couples we are selling to.

If the team wants Noor to feel less like a person pretending to be human, the Sutradhar is the strongest alternative. The gathbandhan knot is ownable, works for every family and community, and says what Noor does: hold every thread of the wedding together.

The Masi has the most charm and is worth testing with couples, but “the aunt” can read as interference to a generation planning their own wedding.

Next steps

  1. Choose the relationship: friend, aunt, thread-holder, concierge or star-reader. The drawing follows from it.
  2. Read the five answers aloud as a team. The voice that sounds most like the brand usually settles it.
  3. Test two personas with couples, using the same question, before an illustrator finishes the art.
  4. Swap it in. The site already has one portrait, one small mark and one thinking motion, so a new persona replaces those three pieces.
